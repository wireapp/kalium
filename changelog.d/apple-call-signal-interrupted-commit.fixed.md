A call signal left unready when an Apple receiving attempt stopped between its event's crypto commit and the signal's ready mark is made ready by the account's next owner: the app before it replays signals, or an extension scan that reaches the end of the queue without deferrals. This holds within the journal's 3-minute signal retention, so the call can still ring or be reported as missed.

  - ABI: changed (`CallSignalStore.markAllSignalsReady` added; implementations must add it)
  - Source: compatible for callers; implementations add the method
