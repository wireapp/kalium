On Apple, `KaliumShareSender.send` uploads files without holding the account, so the app and the notification extension can take it meanwhile: a push that arrives during a long upload is read, and a call rings. `send` holds the account for a moment to read the conversation, the file rules and where the files go, and again to catch up and send as before. The account lock records whether its holder is an app extension: each time `send` takes the account, it waits up to 20 seconds, and never longer than its budget, for another extension that holds it, and 3 seconds when the app does.

  - ABI: unchanged for `KaliumShare`. Additive for the `@InternalKaliumApi` runtime: `AppleAccountRuntimeOwner.tryAcquire`, `AppleAccountRuntime.use`, `useDatabase` and `useUnheld` take the wait for an app extension as an optional argument.
  - Behavior: `send` returns `OPEN_APP` with `BUSY` when the app holds the account, also when a token refresh during the upload could not take it. Uploads have no budget of their own any more, so `send` no longer returns `UPLOAD_TIMEOUT`.
  - Migration: hosts drop their own `BUSY` retries around `send`, which would upload the files again, hand the share to the app on `BUSY`, and stop handling `UPLOAD_TIMEOUT`.
