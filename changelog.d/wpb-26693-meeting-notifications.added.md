Added `LocalNotification.Meeting.Invite`, `Update` and `Cancel` for active notification observers of each account. Invite/update cards include the meeting title, sender when available, and start/end times. Cancel cards use metadata captured before deletion, including when the account is removed from a meeting conversation. Failed deletions do not emit cancellation cards.

  - ABI: existing conversation notification classes and bulk meeting deletion methods remain available. The meeting notification variants, sender fields on meeting event DTOs and `MeetingDao.getMeetingsByConversationId` are additions. Meeting DTO and receiving `Event.Meeting` constructor/copy signatures change; compiled consumers creating these types must recompile. Custom `MeetingDao` implementations must implement the new query.
  - Source: callers that exhaustively match `LocalNotification` must handle the new `Meeting` variants. Existing conversation notification names remain unchanged.
  - Behavior: ephemeral notifications and regular notification checks are scoped to the owning account session. Meeting cards are not persisted or replayed to later observers; NSE applies meeting effects silently.
  - Migration: update exhaustive notification switches, render the new meeting variants, and recompile consumers that construct meeting DTOs or receiving meeting events. No database schema upgrade is introduced.
