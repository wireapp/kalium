Added `KaliumShare`, an XCFramework for the iOS share extension (ADRs 12 and 13). `KaliumShareSender` lists the conversations an extension may send to and sends text and up to 20 files through the app's sender. `send` checks the files against the account's file rules and uploads them, reporting progress through `KaliumShareProgress`, then holds the account live over the WebSocket to send and returns the alerts owed by events received meanwhile. `KaliumShareConversations.files` and `KaliumShareConversation.acceptsFiles` let the host explain refused files and Cells conversations before sending.

While the app owns the account, `conversations` reports `BUSY` and the extension shares through the app instead. `KaliumShareOutbox.askAppForConversations` and `appConversations` list an account's conversations through the app, and `handOff` and `handOffStatus` give the app a share to store and send. On Apple, `UserSessionScope.serveShareExtension(doorbell)` answers the extension while the app owns an account.

  - ABI: additive. `appleAccountFileKey`, `deleteShareOutbox`, `ShareOutboxServer`, `ShareOutboxSending` and `ShareOutboxFile` are `@InternalKaliumApi`.
  - Behavior: unchanged for the app, whose sending path now runs on the same sender in `:domain:messaging:sending`. `budgetMilliseconds` bounds the live hold; uploads run before it. Once a message is not sent, or sending passes its soft deadline, the remaining messages are stored as failed. The app stores handed-off shares with its composer's use cases and rules, files first, then the text, each under the message ID the extension gave it. Logging out drops the account's outbox.
  - Migration: apps run `serveShareExtension` inside each account's work, live or not, ring it when the extension posts its Darwin notification, and cancel it before releasing the account.
