iOS clients now register with Apple's marketing name as their model, such as "iPhone 16", instead of the hardware board ID ("D47AP"). Devices newer than the built-in list send their model identifier, such as "iPhone19,2"; simulators send the simulated model followed by "Simulator". macOS clients are unchanged.

  - ABI: no change
  - Source: no change
  - Migration: none; only newly registered clients get the new name.
