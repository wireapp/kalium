Added `KaliumNSE`, an XCFramework for the iOS Notification Service Extension. `KaliumNSEReceiver.process` receives a push for an account the app has prepared: it catches up over the WebSocket, as the app does, processes the stored event queue to its end and returns a `KaliumNSEResult` with the notification decision. It receives Proteus and MLS text, edits, deletes, delete-for-me, reactions, ephemeral text, conversation renames, receipt modes, message timers, and new groups and channels with their MLS welcomes. Calling signals run through AVS, and `calls` returns the incoming, missed and closed call decisions for the host's CallKit integration. `alerts` returns other unread messages stored without an alert, `presentGenericAlert` asks the host for a generic alert when receiving ran out of time or failed to catch up before the push's event, and `stoppedReason` reports why receiving stopped.

  - ABI: additive
  - Behavior: unchanged for the app. Events the NSE does not handle stay queued for the app's incremental sync. Unread messages the app received without marking them notified, up to six hours old, alert on the next push.
  - Migration: hosts report `calls`, then post `alerts` as local notifications before completing the request. While running, the app marks conversations notified as the Android app does, including the open conversation.
