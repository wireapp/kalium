# Kalium network and sync fixes: developer handoff

Base commit: `7026a46`. Every item below was checked against the source at the quoted line on 2026-10-01. Ktor claims were checked against Ktor 3.5.0 sources. The CoreCrypto transaction lock was checked against `keystore/src/connection/mod.rs` in `wireapp/core-crypto` ("ensures at most one transaction is in flight against this database at a time").

Severity is the impact of the bug today. Lines are the estimated production diff, excluding tests. Items are grouped by effort and ordered smallest first within each group. Each one is intended to be its own PR.

Full reasoning and the longer list of lower-value items are in `network-performance-and-protocol-review.md` in this folder.

| T3-1 | High | `logic/.../sync/incremental/IncrementalSyncWorker.kt:77-107` | A batch of up to 500 events is one `withContext(NonCancellable)` plus one crypto transaction. `foldToEitherWhileRight` aborts on the first `Left`, nothing is marked processed, `KaliumSyncException` is thrown, sync backs off to 10 min, re-reads the same batch and fails again. ## Suggested sequencing

1. Tier 1 in any order. Each is independent, each is a small PR. T1-1 and T1-2 first because they are user-visible failures.
2. T2-1, T2-2, T2-3 together as "slow sync and catch-up cost". T2-6 and T2-7 together as "transport resilience". T2-5 on its own. T2-8 through T2-11 as capacity allows.
3. T3-1 and T3-2 next, they are the two that turn transient failures into permanent ones.
4. T3-5 last, after T3-1 and T3-2 give a stable base and a concurrency test exists.

## What was dropped during validation

These appeared in the earlier review but are left out here as low value or not re-verified: orphaned backend assets on cancel, the Cells upload progress coroutine per tick, the unused `EventProcessingHistory`, `MockWebSocketSession` in `commonMain`, the dead JSON envelope model in `MessageApiV0`, per-request `Url` parsing in `WireDefaultRequest`, the 1 min OkHttp keep-alive, and the process-global `Authenticator.setDefault`. All real, none worth a ticket on their own.
